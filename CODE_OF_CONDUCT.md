# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of
background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code with empathy, precision, and respect.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent while addressing real impact.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on evidence, trade-offs, and the work.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licensing, and responsible-disclosure boundaries.
- Help make participation accessible to people with different levels of familiarity with the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, personal
attacks, or publishing another person's private information are not acceptable in any project space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation
when behavior is unsafe or disruptive. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's
GitHub profile. Security vulnerabilities must use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not
a public conduct report.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and public spaces where a
person is representing the project.
